# User Research

## Goal and method

Validate the hypotheses behind the requirements with real users and adjust the requirements to the findings.

Semi-structured interviews of 20–30 minutes, in person or online. Interviewees are identified by code only (I-01, I-02, …).

| Profile | Target |
|---|---|
| Organizers (Org Admins and event organizers) | At least 3, from at least 2 organizations |
| Students who attend events | At least 3 |

## Interview guide: organizers

1. Tell me about the last event your organization ran. How did you plan it?
2. Which tools did you use for signups, communication and attendance? What went wrong?
3. How does your team split responsibilities? How do you know who is doing what?
4. Have you run events with several parts or days? How did people sign up?
5. What happens when an event is full, or when someone cancels?
6. Have you run events only for members or invited people? How did you control access?
7. How do you tell participants about changes?
8. How do you record attendance? Do you collect feedback, and what do you do with it?
9. Does anyone need to approve an event before it is announced?
10. What information do you need after an event?
11. Have people shown up without signing up? How did you handle it?
12. In competitions, do people sign up alone or as teams?

## Interview guide: participants

1. How do you find out about student events today?
2. Tell me about the last event you signed up for. How did it go?
3. Have you missed an event because it was full or you heard about it too late?
4. At events with several parts or days, did you attend all of them? Why or why not?
5. How would you like to hear about changes and reminders?
6. Have you signed up for something and not gone? What happened?
7. Would you give feedback after an event? What would stop you?
8. How do you decide whether an event is relevant to you?
9. What would make you attend more events?
